Programme Overview

The M.A. in Criminology and Criminal Justice is a 1-year postgraduate programme designed to build an advanced understanding of crime, criminal justice systems, and responses to crime in a global context.

The programme combines rigorous theoretical foundations with empirical research, enabling learners to critically analyse contemporary issues such as violence, policing, inequality, punishment, and digital transformation in justice systems.

With an interdisciplinary approach spanning criminology, law, sociology, psychology, and social sciences, learners gain the ability to understand crime and justice as globally interconnected phenomena.

Delivered in a 100% online format, the programme connects academic learning with real-world challenges, preparing learners to evaluate policies, contribute to justice systems, and pursue impactful careers or further research.
